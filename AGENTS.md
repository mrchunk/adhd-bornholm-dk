# Working conventions

- This is a separate client project from the Thomas Sørensen clinic site — do not mix code, dependencies, or content between the two.
- Tech stack is not yet chosen (see README.md open decisions). Do not scaffold a framework until that's settled.
- Target audience includes people with ADHD and their families — prioritize plain language (Danish), clear structure, and accessibility (contrast, readable fonts, no unnecessary motion) over decorative complexity.
- Site content should be in Danish unless told otherwise.
